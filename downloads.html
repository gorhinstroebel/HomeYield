A few things worth knowing.

Your garden stays on the device where you create it. Browser records and native app records are separate; they do not automatically sync. Weather and release checks need an internet connection.

Only published release assets become direct download buttons. Check the release notes for signing status and SHA-256 checksums before installing; unsigned preview builds may show operating-system warnings. Do not bypass a warning for a file you do not trust.
